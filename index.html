Brass dallah, rebuilt from a single plate

No model file, no photogrammetry, no image textures on the finish: a 3174×3539 CC0 photograph, measured, turned into an ObjectSculptSpec, and generated as lathe and sweep geometry.

ReferenceDallah Syria, Wikimedia Commons, CC0. Brass colour and roughness sampled from it; the extracted maps were re-graded because de-lighting collapsed the metal to olive.
